What moves an option's price

An option's price depends on four inputs you can see: where the stock is relative to the strike, how many days are left, implied volatility, and (barely) interest rates. Drag the sliders and watch all four figures respond. The bold line and dot in each figure are your contract; the thin lines are reference contracts for comparison.
